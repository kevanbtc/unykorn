## Revenue Model

### Revenue Streams

#### 1. Platform Launch Fees

**Description**: One-time fees for token/NFT deployment

**Pricing Tiers**:
- Free (testnet): $0
- Basic: $0 (mainnet, limited features)
- Standard: $499 per launch
- Advanced: $1,499 per launch (multi-chain)
- Enterprise: $5,000+ per launch (custom)

**Volume Assumptions**:

| Tier | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Free | 150 | 300 | 500 |
| Basic | 40 | 100 | 200 |
| Standard | 50 | 200 | 400 |
| Advanced | 10 | 100 | 300 |
| Enterprise | 0 | 10 | 50 |
| **Total Launches** | **250** | **710** | **1,450** |

**Revenue Calculation**:
- Year 1: (50 × $499) + (10 × $1,499) = $40k
- Year 2: (200 × $499) + (100 × $1,499) + (10 × $5,000) = $300k
- Year 3: (400 × $499) + (300 × $1,499) + (50 × $5,000) = $900k

- Liquidity operations: 0.5% on adds/removes

**Volume Assumptions**:

## Scenario Analysis

### Base Case (Presented Above)

**Assumptions**:
- Moderate market adoption
- Successful fundraising (Seed + Series A)
- Competitive but not dominant market position
- 30% annual market growth

**Outcome**: Breakeven in Year 4-5, healthy growth trajectory

---

### Bull Case (+50% Revenue)

**Drivers**:
- Strong market adoption and viral growth
- Multiple strategic partnerships accelerating distribution
- Premium pricing power due to differentiation
- Faster-than-expected market expansion

**Adjusted Metrics**:
- Year 3 Revenue: $9.9M (vs. $6.6M base)
- Year 3 Net Income: $1.5M (vs. -$202k base)
- Breakeven: Year 3 (vs. Year 4-5 base)

**Probability**: 25-30%

---

### Bear Case (-30% Revenue)

**Drivers**:
- Crypto bear market reducing demand
- Increased competition and price pressure
- Regulatory headwinds limiting addressable market
- Execution challenges and delays

**Adjusted Metrics**:
- Year 3 Revenue: $4.6M (vs. $6.6M base)
- Year 3 Net Income: -$1.1M (vs. -$202k base)
- Additional funding required in Year 3
- Breakeven: Year 6-7

**Probability**: 20-25%

**Mitigation**: Cost reduction, pivot strategy, extend runway

## Assumptions and Sensitivities

### Key Assumptions

1. **Market Size**: TAM $120M growing 30% annually
2. **Market Share**: 0.1% → 1.7% over 3 years
3. **Pricing**: Standard tier $499, Advanced $1,499, Enterprise $5k+
4. **Conversion**: 20% free to paid conversion
5. **Churn**: 15% annual customer churn
6. **CAC**: $500 Year 1 declining to $200 Year 3
7. **Gross Margin**: 80-88%
8. **Team Size**: 5 → 35 over 3 years
9. **Fundraising**: $2M Seed, $10M Series A
10. **Macro**: Stable crypto market, no major regulatory disruptions

### Sensitivity Analysis

**Revenue Sensitivity**:
- 10% change in pricing: ±$660k Year 3 revenue
- 10% change in volume: ±$660k Year 3 revenue
- 10% change in conversion rate: ±$400k Year 3 revenue

**Cost Sensitivity**:
- 10% change in salaries: ±$450k Year 3 costs
- 10% change in CAC: ±$150k Year 3 costs
- 10% change in infrastructure: ±$35k Year 3 costs

**Break-Even Sensitivity**:
- 10% higher revenue: Breakeven 6 months earlier
- 10% lower costs: Breakeven 6 months earlier
- Combined (10% revenue up + 10% costs down): Breakeven 12 months earlier

## Conclusion

Unykorn's financial projections demonstrate a viable path to building a sustainable, profitable business in the token launch infrastructure market. Key success factors include:

1. **Execution on Product Roadmap**: Deliver features that attract and retain customers
2. **Efficient Capital Deployment**: Maintain strong unit economics and capital efficiency
3. **Market Positioning**: Establish strong brand and differentiation
4. **Partnership Development**: Leverage white-label and ecosystem partnerships for distribution
5. **Operational Discipline**: Control costs while scaling effectively

With successful execution and favorable market conditions, Unykorn is positioned to achieve profitability within 4-5 years and build a market-leading platform in the multi-billion dollar tokenization opportunity.
