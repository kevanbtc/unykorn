# Use of Proceeds

*Allocation of Capital and Resource Deployment Strategy*

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines how funds raised through token sales, equity financing, and platform revenue will be allocated to support the development, launch, and growth of the Unykorn platform.

**Guiding Principles**:
- **Value Creation**: Prioritize investments with highest ROI and strategic impact
- **Transparency**: Clear reporting on fund usage and outcomes
- **Flexibility**: Adapt allocation based on market conditions and opportunities
- **Efficiency**: Maximize output per dollar spent
- **Sustainability**: Build toward self-funding operations

## Capital Efficiency Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators

**Efficiency Metrics**:
- **Cost per Launch**: Total spend / number of token launches
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Marketing spend / new customers
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Average revenue per customer over lifetime
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Target 3:1 or higher
- **Burn Multiple**: Net burn / net new ARR (target <1.5x)

**Milestone Targets**:

| Metric | Seed Stage | Series A |
|--------|------------|----------|
| Cost per Launch | $10,000 | $2,500 |
| CAC | $500 | $200 |
| LTV | $2,000 | $1,500 |
| LTV/CAC | 4:1 | 7.5:1 |
| Burn Multiple | 3x | 1.2x |

### Budget Management

**Monthly Review**:
- Actual vs. budget variance analysis
- Reforecasting based on actuals
- Reallocation between categories (within 10% without approval)
- Board reporting on material variances

**Quarterly Planning**:
- Rolling 4-quarter budget updates
- Strategic initiative prioritization
- Headcount planning and approval
- Capital expenditure review

**Annual Process**:
- Comprehensive budget development
- Board approval of annual plan
- Departmental allocation and targets
- Hiring and compensation planning

## Alternative Scenarios

### Conservative Case (50% of Target Funding)

**Adjustments**:
- Smaller team (3 core members vs. 5)
- Slower development timeline (+6 months to launch)
- Limited marketing (organic only)
- Single chain focus initially
- Minimal third-party services

**Impact**:
- Longer time to market
- Higher execution risk
- Reduced competitive positioning
- Delayed breakeven (Month 30 vs. 24)

### Accelerated Case (150% of Target Funding)

**Opportunities**:
- Larger team (8-10 members)
- Faster development (6 months to launch)
- Aggressive marketing and growth
- Multi-chain from day one
- Enterprise features accelerated

**Impact**:
- Earlier market entry
- Stronger competitive position
- Higher burn but faster revenue
- Earlier breakeven (Month 18)

## Governance and Accountability

### Spending Authority

**Delegated Authority**:
- <$10k: Department managers
- $10k-$50k: Executive team
- $50k-$250k: CEO with CFO approval
- >$250k: Board approval required

### Audit and Controls

**Internal Controls**:
- Segregation of duties (approval vs. execution)
- Dual signatures for material expenditures
- Monthly reconciliation and variance analysis
- Expense policy and compliance training

**External Audit**:
- Annual financial audit (once at scale)
- Periodic internal audit of controls
- Compliance audits for regulated activities
- Third-party verification of on-chain funds

### Clawback and Recovery

**Misuse Provisions**:
- Recovery of funds spent outside approved budget
- Clawback of bonuses if fraud or misrepresentation
- Legal action for material breaches
- Transparent disclosure of any incidents
